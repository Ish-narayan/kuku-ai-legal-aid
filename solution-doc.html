Problem 2 • AI Lawyer prototype

Why this problem

I chose Problem 2 because it serves people immediately and has clear, checkable outputs: a factual case summary and a usable notice draft. It also makes the model-safety trade-off visible.

Scope & assumptions

Supported now: rental deposits, unpaid salary and online refunds. Language: English and Hindi. Assumption: a user wants a low-risk first step, not guaranteed legal outcomes or court representation.

How it works

  1. The user chooses a problem and enters amount plus start date.
  2. The app collects evidence by category and builds a structured case summary.
  3. It returns plain-language next steps, a realistic forum, a warning, and a copyable notice draft.
  4. Unsupported areas are kept out of scope. Nothing is stored outside the browser.

Prototype design

A chat-style React interface uses a deterministic rules engine. This makes drafts predictable and avoids inventing statutes or cases. The architecture is API-ready: an AdvisorAdapter can replace local templates with a constrained model call, with output validated by the same schema.

Safety

  • No statute or precedent is cited unless verified by a reviewed source.
  • The UI states that it is not legal advice.
  • Lawyer escalation is explicit for filing or deadline-sensitive matters.
  • The notice includes facts supplied by the user, never implied facts.

What a production version should ground with

Curated, jurisdiction-aware sources: India Code / state acts, consumer and labour portals, court procedure, Legal Services Authorities, and local advocate directories. The model should receive only retrieved passages and must output: facts, uncertainties, options, evidence, source links and escalation status. A human/legal content reviewer should approve templates and source updates.

Prototype is an informational aid, not a substitute for a licensed Indian legal professional.

Quality & scale

Evidence-based validation

Runs performed

Three test cases cover each supported category, with English/Hindi interface intent and unedited raw outputs in raw-outputs/test-cases.json. The app was compiled successfully with Vite. Templates preserve user-entered amount, date and deadline, and no unsupported legal claim is generated.

Where it works

  • Fast initial triage
  • Evidence checklist
  • Readable notice draft
  • Clear lawyer-disclaimer
  • Hindi/English switch

Where it fails or is limited

  • Cannot assess jurisdiction or contract terms deeply
  • Does not handle voice or real chat yet
  • No document OCR or verification
  • Not a filing or deadline calculator
  • Outside three flows, it should route to escalation rather than guess

Scaling to real volume

Stateless core: category rules and retrieval are separated from UI. Cache source passages and notice templates. Queue document extraction and model calls. Store consented case data only; avoid collecting Aadhaar/bank secrets. Add logs for escalation, incomplete evidence and model refusals. The local prototype costs near zero; a production model call can be limited to unstructured fact extraction and checked drafting, with retrieval and validation in code.

How success would be measured

≥90%field extraction accuracy on agreed test cases
<5%unsupported legal claims in audited replies
50%+of users report a clear next action

Human-in-the-loop

Source review, template approval and escalation to a licensed lawyer remain mandatory. The app makes “I don't know” an explicit outcome.

Tools used

React, Vite, TypeScript, Lucide icons, and AI-assisted implementation. Where AI got it wrong: an initial dependency setup assumed a server framework and was replaced with a simple static frontend. No tool-generated legal claim is treated as authoritative.
